How it works

  1. After a session, type a few rough bullet points or dictate them.
  2. The AI, running on your own computer, turns them into a clear, structured note in the style you prefer.
  3. You review, edit and save. Notes are stored encrypted on your device, and you can export them to PDF or paste them into your practice system.
